2. Data Types and Required Permissions

CleanBoost is an application designed for storage optimization and file management on Android devices. To perform its core features, it requires the following permissions on your device:

  • Storage Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): This permission is strictly used to scan local device storage to detect temporary files, residual cache, and duplicate files.

Local Data Processing: All file scanning and processing occur exclusively offline on the user's device. CleanBoost does not collect, store, upload to external servers, or share any personal files (such as photos, videos, documents, contacts, or messages).

3. In-App Purchases and Payment Management

CleanBoost offers advanced features through In-App purchases or subscriptions (PRO Version):

  • Google Play Billing: All transactions and payments are securely and exclusively handled by official Google Play Store services. The application and the Developer do not have access to, collect, or store any financial details, credit card numbers, or banking information.

4. Third-Party Services

The application only utilizes system libraries necessary for proper operational performance:

  • Google Play Services / Google Play Billing: Used to verify the application license and manage PRO version purchases.

5. Data Retention and Security

Since all scanning and cleanup operations are executed solely on the user's local device, no personal data is retained or stored on external servers or databases.

6. User Rights

Users maintain full control over the permissions granted to the application:

  • You can revoke storage access at any time via your Android operating system menu: Settings > Apps > CleanBoost > Permissions.
  • You can uninstall the application at any time to stop all scanning operations.
